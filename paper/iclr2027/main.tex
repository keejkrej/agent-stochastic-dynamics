\begin{abstract}
The process is a closed loop: self-observe $\to$ self-improve $\to$ self-observe $\to\cdots$. A virtual-DOM (vdom) agent reads its own traces, writes a critique into memory ($\mathrm{Obs}$), and applies $P^{\mathrm{ctrl}}(\cdot\mid\mathrm{Obs}(\mathrm{traces}))$. That kernel is iterated on the fast clock. $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$ mutates the AgentGraph, so a deterministic reconciler changes the composition of the serving kernel $K_C$. $I_{\mathrm{weight}}$ dispatches an asynchronous trainer; serving keeps the old $f_\theta$; a gated mount is a jump on the slow clock. After either intervention the agent serves again, $\mathrm{Obs}$ reads the \emph{new} traces, and $P^{\mathrm{ctrl}}$ may fire again. $\mathrm{wait}$ is a fixed point. We write this on the hybrid state $X=(H,M,E,C)$ with typed noise (sampling, numerical, environmental) and two clocks. The eval functional is $\mathrm{pass}^k$: first-passage under $k$ i.i.d.\ repeats. The figure is the loop---$\mathrm{pass}^k(t)$ versus cycle $t$---not a one-shot before/after and not a static one-shot score. A live diagnostic licenses $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$ first; a five-task retail slice already saturates at $\mathrm{pass}^k=1.0$ under technique \texttt{one-shot} (a ceiling, not the loop). Cycle scores are not invented.
\end{abstract}

\section{The claim}

The process is a \emph{closed loop}:
\[
\text{self-observe}\;\to\;\text{self-improve}\;\to\;\text{self-observe}\;\to\;\cdots
\]
A one-shot pair $(\mathrm{Obs},I)$ is one edge of that loop, not the object. A static $\mathrm{pass}^k=1.0$ on a one-shot graph is not the object. After $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$ or a gated $I_{\mathrm{weight}}$ mount, serving produces new traces; $\mathrm{Obs}$ reads them; $P^{\mathrm{ctrl}}$ may intervene again.

An LLM agent is usually described as a generator with tools, or as a latent diffusion, or as an MDP over memory operations. Those descriptions miss the loop. The agent \emph{reads its own traces}. It writes a critique of its path measure into structured memory ($\mathrm{Obs}$). Control is the iteration
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ctrl}
C_{n+1}\;\sim\; P^{\mathrm{ctrl}}\big(\cdot\mid C_n,\,\mathrm{Obs}(\mathrm{traces}_n)\big)
\end{equation}
on the \textbf{fast clock}. The two arms that change the path measure are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Loop.} Emit a new AgentGraph $G'$. Reconcile is a deterministic map $(G,G')\mapsto$ mount / update / retain / unmount. The \emph{composition} of $K$ changes: critics, refiners, validators. $f_\theta$ does not. Then serve; observe again.
\item \textbf{Weights.} Dispatch an asynchronous trainer. Serving does not stop (old $f_\theta$). When the artifact is ready and an eval gate passes, reconcile rebinds \texttt{PhysicalNode.provider}: a jump on the \textbf{slow clock}. Failed eval: no switch. Later regression: unmount. Then serve; observe the new traces; $P^{\mathrm{ctrl}}$ may fire again.
\end{enumerate}
$\mathrm{wait}$ is the identity on $C$: a fixed point of~\eqref{eq:ctrl}. Topology is a value \citep{vdomharness}. Determinism is allocated on the fast clock; learning is gated on the slow clock.

\paragraph{Two artifacts.}
Theory, typed-noise kernel, experiments, and this draft live in \texttt{agent-stochastic-dynamics}. The accompanying runtime \citep{vdomharness} iterates \texttt{improveLoop} given $\mathrm{Obs}$. Papers, blogs, GitHub, and other agents are inputs to that runtime, not the product.

\paragraph{What we are not claiming.}
Not Langevin on the loop, not an SDE on a latent manifold. Generation-SDE papers \citep{sde2408,sde2510,lgoc2605} model token generation. Memory-as-MDP papers \citep{slmv3,memcon,memento2,memp} enlarge memory actions. Reflexion and Self-Refine \citep{reflexion,selfrefine} are particular $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$ mutations, typically one pass, without weight updates and without iterating $\mathrm{Obs}$ after a slow-clock jump. The gap is the closed loop~\eqref{eq:ctrl} on a typed-noise hybrid kernel, with $I_{\mathrm{weight}}$ jumps off the fast clock.

\section{Hybrid state and typed noise}
\label{sec:hybrid}

\begin{definition}[Hybrid state]
At serving step $n$,
\[
X_n=(H_n,M_n,E_n,C_n)\in\mathcal{V}^*\times\mathcal{M}\times\mathcal{E}\times\mathcal{C},
\]
where $H$ is context, $M$ is structured memory (including $\mathrm{Obs}$ critiques), $E$ is the environment / tools, and $C$ is the AgentGraph plus decoding knobs and bound model / adapter ids. An optional embedding $z_n=\Pi(X_n)$ is a projection, never the state.
\end{definition}

One serving step is a composition, not a diffusion:
(i) $a_{n+1}\sim P^{\mathrm{gen}}_C(\cdot\mid H_n,M_n)$;
(ii) $(E_{n+1},o_{n+1})\sim P^{\mathrm{env}}(\cdot\mid E_n,a_{n+1})$;
(iii) $M_{n+1}\sim P^{\mathrm{mem}}_C(\cdot\mid M_n,H_n,a_{n+1},o_{n+1})$;
(iv) $H_{n+1}=\mathrm{append}(H_n,a_{n+1},o_{n+1})$ (Dirac);
(v) $C_{n+1}\sim P^{\mathrm{ctrl}}(\cdot\mid C_n,\mathrm{Obs}(\mathrm{traces}_n))$ --- \emph{iterated}, not a one-shot map.
Write $X_{n+1}\sim K_C(\cdot\mid X_n)$. Time is discrete and event-driven. The pair $(X_n)$ is Markov on $\mathcal{X}$ because $C_n\subset X_n$.

Generation factors as
\[
P^{\mathrm{gen}}(a\mid H,M,C)=\int P^{\mathrm{dec}}_C(a\mid \tilde\ell)\,P^{\mathrm{num}}(d\tilde\ell\mid f_\theta(H),C).
\]
Three channels, conditionally independent given $X_n$:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Sampling} $\xi^{\mathrm{samp}}$: designed decoding RNG. Vanishes as $\tau\to 0$ with unique argmax.
\item \textbf{Numerical} $\xi^{\mathrm{num}}$: logit perturbation $\tilde\ell=f_\theta(H)+\varepsilon$, not white in time. A boundary layer on near-ties, not a diffusion coefficient (Lemma~\ref{lem:gap}).
\item \textbf{Environmental} $\xi^{\mathrm{env}}$: tools, web, clocks, other agents. Discrete, heavy-tailed.
\end{itemize}

\section{The self-observe / self-improve loop}
\label{sec:obs}

\begin{definition}[Observation]
$\mathrm{Obs}$ maps traces and first-passage proxies $(\hat\tau_S,\hat\tau_F,\hat p_{\mathrm{hit}})$ to a critique in $M$:
\[
M \leftarrow M \cup \{\texttt{obs}: \mathrm{Obs}(\mathrm{traces})\}.
\]
It is a statistic, not a fourth noise channel. In the runtime: \texttt{traces} plus \texttt{runBenchmark.score} \citep{vdomharness}. After every intervention the same operator is applied to the \emph{new} traces.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Closed loop]
\label{def:loop}
Index improvement cycles by $t=0,1,2,\ldots$ (a subsample of the fast clock, or a wall-clock between evals). At cycle $t$ the agent serves under $C_t$, producing $\mathrm{traces}_t$ and $\mathrm{pass}^k(t)$. Then
\[
C_{t+1}\;\sim\; P^{\mathrm{ctrl}}\big(\cdot\mid C_t,\,\mathrm{Obs}(\mathrm{traces}_t)\big).
\]
Support: $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$, $I_{\mathrm{weight}}$ (spawn), gated mount, rollback, capability mount, commit, $\mathrm{wait}$. $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$ changes the composition of $K$ on the fast clock. $I_{\mathrm{weight}}$ spawn does not mutate $C$; a jump $f_\theta\mapsto f_{\theta'}$ occurs only at a gated mount on the slow clock. Then the agent serves under $C_{t+1}$, $\mathrm{Obs}$ reads $\mathrm{traces}_{t+1}$, and the kernel is applied again. The diagram is
\[
\mathrm{traces}_t \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Obs}} M \xrightarrow{P^{\mathrm{ctrl}}} C_{t+1} \xrightarrow{K_{C_{t+1}}} \mathrm{traces}_{t+1} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Obs}} \cdots
\]
This is the figure. A single before/after is one arrow, not the loop.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Two clocks]
The \textbf{fast clock} $n\in\mathbb{N}$ is serving under $K_C$ and the iteration~\eqref{eq:ctrl}. Sampling and environmental noise live here. $\theta$ is frozen until a slow jump. The \textbf{slow clock} $\sigma\in\mathbb{N}$ is trainer jobs. Job $\sigma$ consumes traces and returns an artifact $A_\sigma$ at an optional time $T_{\mathrm{adapt}}$. Serving does not block. On $\{T_{\mathrm{adapt}}=n\}$ a gate $g(A_\sigma,\text{fixture})\in\{\mathrm{mount},\mathrm{reject}\}$ is an empirical first-passage test. After a mount, $\mathrm{Obs}$ is not done: it reads the post-jump traces and~\eqref{eq:ctrl} continues. The fast process is piecewise homogeneous between slow jumps.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[First passage and $\mathrm{pass}^k(t)$]
\label{def:passk}
Success set $S$, failure $F$, $\tau_S=\inf\{n:X_n\in S\}$, $\tau_F=\inf\{n:X_n\in F\}$, $p_{\mathrm{hit}}=\mathbb{P}(\tau_S<\tau_F)$. Under $k$ i.i.d.\ repeats,
\[
\mathrm{pass}^k=\mathbb{P}\big(\tau_S^{(i)}<\tau_F^{(i)}\ \forall i\le k\big)=(p_{\mathrm{hit}})^k
\]
if the repeats are independent. This is \emph{not} $\mathrm{pass}@k$. Along the closed loop write $\mathrm{pass}^k(t)$ at cycle $t$. The figure is the sequence $(\mathrm{pass}^k(t))_{t\ge 0}$, not a lone $\Delta^k$. One-step differences $\mathrm{pass}^k(t+1)-\mathrm{pass}^k(t)$ are edges of the loop. The $I_{\mathrm{weight}}$ mount gate reads $\mathrm{pass}^k$ (or fixture $\hat p_{\mathrm{hit}}$), not a single lucky rollout.
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Cascade.}
TV Lipschitz of any kernel is $\le 1$. Expansion after a one-bit memory split is visible in Hamming on prefixes. Autoregression plus memory write is typically expanding until a commit or a validator contracts.

\section{Lemmas}
\label{sec:lemmas}

\begin{lemma}[Greedy collapse]
If $\tau=0$, the argmax of $f_\theta(H)$ is unique a.s., numerical noise is absent, and env / memory / control are Dirac, then $K_C(\cdot\mid x)=\delta_{\Phi(x)}$. Residual randomness is environmental or a numerical tie---or a loop in the policy, not designed noise.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Gap-sensitivity]\label{lem:gap}
Let $\Delta\ell=\ell_{(1)}-\ell_{(2)}$ and $\tilde\ell=\ell+\varepsilon$. If $\Delta\ell>2\|\varepsilon\|_\infty$ then $\arg\max\tilde\ell=\arg\max\ell$. Hence $\mathbb{P}(\mathrm{flip})\le\mathbb{P}(\Delta\ell\le 2\|\varepsilon\|_\infty)$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Spawn $\neq$ mount]\label{lem:spawn}
Spawning a trainer does not mutate $C$. While the job runs, the serving process is Markov under the pre-job kernel $K_C$, and~\eqref{eq:ctrl} may still apply $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$ or $\mathrm{wait}$ on the fast clock. A jump in $f_\theta$ occurs only at a gated mount. After that jump, $\mathrm{Obs}$ is applied to the new traces. Reject and rollback are the identity (resp.\ the inverse) on the jump.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Eval gate]
Mounts occur iff $\hat p_{\mathrm{hit}}\ge\mathrm{threshold}$ on a fixture (or $\mathrm{pass}^k$ on $\tau^2$-bench when that is the gate). This is empirical first-passage (Definition~\ref{def:passk}), not a new objective.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Wait is a fixed point]
If $\mathrm{Obs}(\mathrm{traces}_t)$ selects $\mathrm{wait}$, then $C_{t+1}=C_t$ and the fast kernel is unchanged. The closed loop idles until traces change (new tasks, a slow-clock mount, or env noise) enough for $\mathrm{Obs}$ to leave $\mathrm{wait}$.
\end{lemma}

Proofs of the first two are elementary. Lemma~\ref{lem:spawn} is by construction of $I_{\mathrm{weight}}$. Further tradeoffs: \texttt{paper/FRAMEWORK.md}. The 0731 counts that \emph{license} $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$ first---not the closed-loop figure---are in \texttt{paper/ANALYSIS.md}.

\section{The result: the loop, not a one-shot pair}
\label{sec:result}

No private corpus. Live sampling, when keyed, uses OpenRouter and \texttt{deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-0731} only. We do not invent $\tau^2$ scores.

The \emph{result} is the closed loop of Definition~\ref{def:loop}: $\mathrm{pass}^k(t)$ as $P^{\mathrm{ctrl}}(\cdot\mid\mathrm{Obs})$ iterates. Not a static $\mathrm{pass}^k$. Not a single before/after.

\subsection{Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Start naive.} Mount a one-shot AgentGraph $C_0$ (\texttt{oneShotGraph} / \texttt{technique: one-shot}).
\item \textbf{Cycle $t=0,1,\ldots,T$.} Serve on a $\tau^2$ slice that is \emph{not} already saturated. Record $\mathrm{pass}^k(t)$ from official \texttt{compute\_metrics}. Write $\mathrm{traces}_t$ into $M$.
\item \textbf{$\mathrm{Obs}(\mathrm{traces}_t)$.} Repeat rate, timeout, zero progress, tool failures, knowledge-miss, $\hat p_{\mathrm{hit}}$.
\item \textbf{$P^{\mathrm{ctrl}}$.} $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$ (\texttt{improveLoop} topology: critic / refine / validator) or $I_{\mathrm{weight}}$ (spawn; later gated mount). Or $\mathrm{wait}$ (fixed point; stop). Serving does not block on the trainer.
\item \textbf{Do not stop after one intervention.} Serve under $C_{t+1}$. $\mathrm{Obs}$ reads the new traces. Repeat from (2).
\end{enumerate}

Implementation: \url{https://github.com/keejkrej/vdom-harness}. \texttt{improveLoop} already iterates given $\mathrm{Obs}$ (\texttt{maxIters}). Official $\tau^2$ owns domains, tools, user simulator, and $\mathrm{pass}^k$. \texttt{python -m tau2\_vdom} registers \texttt{--agent vdom}:

\begin{verbatim}
# Cycle t. Unsaturated slice -- not the 5-task retail at pass^k = 1.0.
PYTHONPATH=python python3 -m tau2_vdom \
  --domain <airline|telecom|larger-retail> \
  --task-ids <fixed ids> --num-trials k

# Obs(traces_t) -> P^ctrl: improveLoop (topology = I_loop;
# adapter spawn / gated mount = I_weight). Then cycle t+1
# on the SAME task ids. Stop only on wait or t = T.
\end{verbatim}

Copy official \texttt{compute\_metrics} per cycle. Do not invent $\mathrm{pass}^k(t)$.

\subsection{Ceiling on an easy slice (do not lead with this)}
\label{sec:ceiling}

A live official retail slice already exists: $N=5$ tasks $\times$ $4$ trials, \texttt{technique: one-shot}, OpenRouter \texttt{deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-0731}, copied from vdom-harness \texttt{eval/tau2/retail-live-metrics.json} into \texttt{experiments/tau2-retail-0731.json}. Official \texttt{compute\_metrics}: $\mathrm{avgReward}=1.0$, $\mathrm{pass}^1=\mathrm{pass}^2=\mathrm{pass}^3=\mathrm{pass}^4=1.0$. Not invented. Small slice only. No larger $\tau^2$ table invented.

That run is a static one-shot score. $\mathrm{Obs}$ selects $\mathrm{wait}$; the loop is already at a fixed point. It is a ceiling on an easy slice. It does not demonstrate the closed loop. The protocol needs a slice with $\mathrm{pass}^k(0)<1$ so~\eqref{eq:ctrl} has something to iterate.

\paragraph{Why the first $P^{\mathrm{ctrl}}$ should pick $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$.}
Word-reverse and calculator sit in temperature-invariant action attractors (consecutive-repeat rate $60/60=1$). Calculator's first tool is already correct ($24/24$); $M$ contains the write of $8$; generation does not consume it. Retrieval hits ($12/12$, $\tau_S=1$). $\tau=0$ already fails; $\tau=0.7$ does not change $p_{\mathrm{hit}}$ on the failures. Numerical tags: $0/312$. By greedy collapse, residual randomness is a loop in the policy. A one-bit memory coupling sometimes leaves the attractor; temperature never does. Therefore cycle $t=0$ emits $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$. After that mutation, $\mathrm{Obs}$ must read the new traces: if the attractor remains, iterate $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$ or spawn $I_{\mathrm{weight}}$. That second look is the closed loop; the $0/12$ alone is not.

\section{Discussion}

The contribution is the self-observe / self-improve loop: iterate $P^{\mathrm{ctrl}}(\cdot\mid\mathrm{Obs}(\mathrm{traces}))$ on the fast clock, with $I_{\mathrm{weight}}$ jumps on the slow clock. Theory names $X=(H,M,E,C)$, $K_C$, three channels, two clocks, $\mathrm{pass}^k(t)$, and the lemmas. Everything else is a license or a ceiling.

The 0731 diagnostic buys the first arm: $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$ before $I_{\mathrm{weight}}$ on sequential-tool attractors. The five-task retail $1.0$ buys a warning: $\mathrm{wait}$ on a saturated slice is a fixed point, not a result.

In the accompanying runtime \citep{vdomharness}, \texttt{improveLoop} already iterates. Keep $\mathrm{Obs}$ a first-class memory write after every cycle, including after a gated mount. Keep the trainer out-of-process. Fill the cycle table of \S\ref{sec:torun} from official $\tau^2$ JSON. Papers, blogs, GitHub, and other agents remain inputs, not the product.

\section{Conclusion}

A vdom agent watches itself and then edits its loop or dispatches a weight update that must not interrupt serving---and then watches itself again. That closed loop is the paper. Theory: hybrid kernel, typed noise, two clocks, spawn $\neq$ mount, $\mathrm{wait}$ as fixed point. The 0731 toys license the first $I_{\mathrm{loop}}$. The figure is $\mathrm{pass}^k(t)$ on the loop, not a one-shot score and not a one-shot pair.
